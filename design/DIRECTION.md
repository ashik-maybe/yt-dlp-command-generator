# Direction

Five lines. The design system and all copy follow them.

Product: turns a link into one pasteable yt-dlp line for a person who never opened a terminal.

Character: plain, exact, a little archival.

World: shipping paperwork, paper, ink, rubber stamps, dotted leaders.

Moments: the command receipt and the format ladder.

Signature: a `CHECKED · <version>` stamp on every command.
